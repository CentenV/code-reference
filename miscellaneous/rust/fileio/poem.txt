The wind hums soft, a gentle tune,
Beneath the silver crescent moon.
It dances through the autumn leaves,
And weaves a spell the heart believes.

The stars, like lanterns, faintly glow,
Illuminating all below.
A river murmurs, cool and deep,
Its secrets buried, fast asleep.

The earth is still, the night is wide,
With dreams that on the breeze collide.
In every shadow, whispers play,
And guide the soul to break of day.
